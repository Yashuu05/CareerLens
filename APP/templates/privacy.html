Privacy Policy

Important Notice: This project is not made for production. It is built to showcase skills such as Machine Learning and MLOps, and as a trial to build end-to-end software. This platform is a college project and may not be accurate at prediction and calculation. Therefore, users should not blindly believe the calculations, prediction and roadmap.

1. Information Collected

We collect personal information that you provide to us to better serve your career growth. The data we gather includes:

  • Names and Email addresses
  • Age and demographic information
  • Career details, academic backgrounds, and skill sets

2. Collection Methods

We obtain your data primarily through user accounts and contact forms. When you sign up and fill out details in your profile, this project securely collects your data through forms and saves it safely to MongoDB Atlas. Users are authorized securely through JWT authentication.

3. Purpose of Use

We collect your data exclusively for processing, prediction, and calculation purposes. This allows us to provide personalized career insights, skill gap analysis, and roadmap generations.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We never sell any user data to third-party software. MongoDB Atlas and Google Gemini API are the only two third-party services used. We may collect and process data, such as Skill Gaps, by sending it to Google Gemini through their API to generate your AI Roadmap.

5. User Rights

You have full control over your personal data. Our platform provides a direct way for you to delete your user data or completely delete your account via the Settings page. You can access and change your personal details at any time.
